03 — SAFETY CHECKLIST

Your pause-before-click playbook.

A simple repeatable checklist. Keep it handy for unexpected messages and requests.

01

Pause the pressure

Be skeptical of urgent deadlines, emotional threats or offers that sound too good to be true.

02

Inspect the destination

Read the real website host, especially when the address uses shorteners, IP numbers or an @ symbol.

03

Verify independently

Open an official app or type the organization's site yourself. Don't call numbers supplied only in the message.

04

Protect your credentials

Never send passwords, one-time codes or sensitive documents in response to unsolicited requests.

05

Ask a trusted person

If you're uncertain, get a second opinion using a separate and trusted communication channel.

06

Act if you already clicked

Stop sharing information. Use the genuine app to reset exposed passwords and contact the provider if needed.

This checklist works without an internet connection.

After your first visit, SafeSignal caches its interface and guidance locally in supported browsers.

04 — DESIGN WITH TRUST

Clarity, not false confidence.

Built for the SafeSpace Hacks 2026 challenge. Transparent technology and clear limits.

⚙

What happens

Small, deterministic rules examine language and visible URL hosts for common warning signs. Every matched rule produces a plain-language explanation.

⌁

What stays private

Your pasted message remains in this browser tab's memory. No cloud classification, sign-in, cookie, analytics, database or saved message history.

◈

What we cannot tell

We don't visit links, check live reputation, identify real senders or scan attachments. Novel scams can be missed and ordinary messages can trigger warnings.

☆

What matters

The safer outcome is a pause to independently verify before clicking or sharing sensitive information, not a numerical prediction.
